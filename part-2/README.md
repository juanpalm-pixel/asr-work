# Part 2: Automatic speech recognition

This exercise introduces automatic speech recognition (ASR) with the multilingual MMS model. Google Colab runs a notebook in your browser and may provide a GPU, so you can try the exercise without setting up a local training environment. GPU availability is not guaranteed.

## Start with the notebook

[**Open the Session 05 MMS ASR notebook in Google Colab**](https://colab.research.google.com/github/juanpalm-pixel/asr-work/blob/main/part-2/notebooks/Session_05_MMS_ASR_Colab.ipynb) ([repository copy](notebooks/Session_05_MMS_ASR_Colab.ipynb)).

The notebook takes you from sample speech data and transcriptions through baseline MMS inference, optional adapter training and evaluation. Follow it for setup, commands and the practical exercises.

The notebook uses public FLEURS samples by default. If you choose your own audio, prepare the audio and transcript CSV as the notebook describes. Obtain permission before uploading recordings to Colab, Google Drive or another service; do not publish private recordings, transcripts or trained weights without authorisation.
